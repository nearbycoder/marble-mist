    MARBLE MIST

    Race ten marbles down floating obstacle courses. Ramps, drops, bumpers, spinners and stompers, and nothing but air past the edges.

    Mode
    Rivals
    Coins
    Drag away from your marble to roll that way (further = faster), or use WASD / arrows (screen directions). Roll off an edge and you lose time respawning at the last checkpoint; drop too far and your marble shatters. Yours is the chrome one.
    Bet mode: you don't steer. Pick a marble, bet on it to win or to finish in the top three, and watch. Better marbles pay less. Coins are kept in this browser.
    Controllers work too: left stick rolls, right stick zooms, A selects, B goes back, Start pauses.
